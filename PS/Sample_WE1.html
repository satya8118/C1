Model Questions

Real Number System · Functions · Limits · Introduction to Riemann Integration

Instructions. Attempt all questions. Notation:

\[ \mathbb{N}=\{1,2,3,\dots\},\qquad \mathbb{Z}=\{\dots,-2,-1,0,1,2,\dots\},\qquad \mathbb{Q}=\text{set of rational numbers},\qquad \mathbb{R}=\text{set of real numbers}. \]

For a set \(S\subseteq \mathbb{R}\), \(\operatorname{lub} S\), \(\operatorname{glb} S\), \(\max S\), and \(\min S\) denote the least upper bound, greatest lower bound, maximum, and minimum of \(S\), respectively.

Part A: Real Number System, Field Axioms, and Order Properties

State the field axioms for \(\mathbb{R}\). Which of these axioms fail for \(\mathbb{N}\)?
Prove that the additive identity \(0\) in \(\mathbb{R}\) is unique.
Prove that the additive inverse of each \(x\in\mathbb{R}\) is unique.
Prove that for every \(x\in\mathbb{R}\), \[ (-1)x=-x. \]
Using the order axioms, prove that if \(x>0\) and \(y>0\), then \(xy>0\).
Prove that if \(x<y\) and \(z\in\mathbb{R}\), then \[ x+z<y+z. \]
Define the absolute value of a real number \(x\) by \[ |x|= \begin{cases} x, & x\ge 0,\\ -x, & x<0. \end{cases} \] Prove that \(|x|\ge 0\) for all \(x\in\mathbb{R}\), and that \(|x|=0\) if and only if \(x=0\).
Prove that for all \(x,y\in\mathbb{R}\), \[ |xy|=|x|\,|y|. \]
Prove that for \(a>0\), \[ |x|<a \iff -a<x<a. \] Hence solve the inequality \[ |2x-3|<5. \]
State and prove the triangle inequality: \[ |x+y|\le |x|+|y| \] for all \(x,y\in\mathbb{R}\).
Prove that every finite nonempty subset of \(\mathbb{R}\) has a maximum and a minimum.
Prove that if \(0<a<b\), then \(a^n<b^n\) for every \(n\in\mathbb{N}\).
For the set \[ S=\left\{1-\frac{1}{n}:n\in\mathbb{N}\right\}, \] find \(\operatorname{lub} S\), \(\operatorname{glb} S\), \(\max S\), and \(\min S\), if they exist.
Let \[ S=\{x\in\mathbb{Q}:x^2<2\}. \] Show that \(S\) has no maximum in \(\mathbb{Q}\). Find \(\operatorname{lub} S\) in \(\mathbb{R}\).
For the set \[ S=\left\{\frac{1}{n}:n\in\mathbb{N}\right\}, \] find \(\operatorname{lub} S\), \(\operatorname{glb} S\), \(\max S\), and \(\min S\), if they exist.
For the set \[ S=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:x^2<5\}, \] find \(\operatorname{lub} S\), \(\operatorname{glb} S\), \(\max S\), and \(\min S\), if they exist.
For the set \[ S=(0,1]\cup\{2\}, \] find \( \operatorname{lub} S\), \(\operatorname{glb} S\), \(\max S\), and \(\min S\), if they exist.
State the least upper bound (lub) property of \(\mathbb{R}\). Use it to prove that every nonempty subset of \(\mathbb{R}\) that is bounded below has a greatest lower bound (glb).
Show that \(\mathbb{Q}\) does not satisfy the lub property by giving an explicit example.
Let \(A,B\subseteq\mathbb{R}\) be nonempty and bounded above. Define \[ A+B=\{a+b:a\in A,\ b\in B\}. \] Prove that \[ \operatorname{lub}(A+B)=\operatorname{lub} A+\operatorname{lub} B. \]
Let \(A\subseteq\mathbb{R}\) be nonempty and bounded. Define \[ -A=\{-a:a\in A\}. \] Prove that \[ \operatorname{lub}(-A)=-\operatorname{glb} A. \]
Prove that between any two real numbers \(x<y\), there exists a rational number \(r\) such that \[ x<r<y. \]
Let \(x\in\mathbb{R}\) and \(\varepsilon>0\). Prove that there exists \(r\in\mathbb{Q}\) such that \[ |x-r|<\varepsilon. \] Explain in words why this means the rational numbers are dense in \(\mathbb{R}\).
State the Archimedean property of \(\mathbb{R}\). Use it to prove that for every \(\varepsilon>0\), there exists \(n\in\mathbb{N}\) such that \[ \frac{1}{n}<\varepsilon. \]
Prove that if \(a\le b+\varepsilon\) for every \(\varepsilon>0\), then \(a\le b\).
For the set \[ S=\{x\in\mathbb{R}:x^3<8\}, \] find \(\operatorname{lub} S\) and determine whether \(S\) has a maximum.
Let \(S\) be a nonempty subset of \(\mathbb{R}\) that is bounded above. Prove that if \(\operatorname{lub} S\in S\), then \(\max S=\operatorname{lub} S\).

Part B: Functions, Composition, Inverse, and Bijections

Define a function \(f:A\to B\). Define its domain, codomain, and range. When are two functions equal?
Let \(f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}\) be defined by \[ f(x)=x^2. \] Find the domain, codomain, and range of \(f\). Is \(f\) injective? Is \(f\) surjective? Is \(f\) bijective?
Let \(f:[0,\infty)\to[0,\infty)\) be defined by \[ f(x)=x^2. \] Show that \(f\) is bijective and find \(f^{-1}\).
Let \(f:\mathbb{R}\to[0,\infty)\) be defined by \[ f(x)=x^2. \] Is \(f\) injective? Is \(f\) surjective? Justify your answer.
Let \(f:\mathbb{Z}\to\mathbb{Z}\) be defined by \[ f(n)=2n. \] Is \(f\) injective? Is \(f\) surjective? Justify your answer.
Let \[ f:\mathbb{R}\setminus\{2\}\to\mathbb{R}\setminus\{1\} \] be defined by \[ f(x)=\frac{x+1}{x-2}. \] Show that \(f\) is bijective and find \(f^{-1}(y)\) for \(y\in\mathbb{R}\setminus\{1\}\).
Let \(f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}\) be defined by \[ f(x)=2x+3. \] Find \(f^{-1}(y)\) for \(y\in\mathbb{R}\). Verify that \(f(f^{-1}(y))=y\).
Let \(f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}\) be defined by \(f(x)=x^2\). Find \[ f^{-1}(4),\qquad f^{-1}(-1),\qquad f^{-1}([0,4]). \]
Let \(f:A\to B\). Prove that \(f\) is bijective if and only if \(f\) has an inverse function \(f^{-1}:B\to A\).
Let \(f:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}\) and \(g:\mathbb{R}\to\mathbb{R}\) be defined by \[ f(x)=x+1,\qquad g(x)=x^2. \] Compute \(f\circ g\) and \(g\circ f\). Are they equal?
Let \[ f(x)=\frac{1}{x},\qquad g(x)=\frac{x}{x+1}. \] Compute \(f\circ g\) and \(g\circ f\). Find their domains.
Let \[ f(x)=\sqrt{x},\qquad g(x)=x^2. \] Compute \(f\circ g\) and \(g\circ f\). Find their domains.
Prove that if \(f\) and \(g\) are injective, then \(f\circ g\) is injective.
If \(f\circ g\) is injective, must \(f\) be injective? Must \(g\) be injective? Give a counterexample if not.
If \(f\circ g\) is surjective, must \(f\) be surjective? Must \(g\) be surjective? Give a counterexample if not.
Let \(f:A\to B\) and \(g:B\to C\) be bijections. Prove that \[ (g\circ f)^{-1}=f^{-1}\circ g^{-1}. \]
Let \[ f(x)=x^2,\qquad g(x)=2x+1. \] Solve the equation \[ (f\circ g)(x)=(g\circ f)(x). \]
Let \(f:A\to B\). Prove that \(f\) is injective if and only if for every \(y\in B\), the set \(f^{-1}(y)\) contains at most one element.

Part C: Limits

Define \[ \lim_{x\to a}f(x)=L \] using the \(\varepsilon\)-\(\delta\) definition.
Prove using the \(\varepsilon\)-\(\delta\) definition that \[ \lim_{x\to 2}(3x+1)=7. \]
Prove using the \(\varepsilon\)-\(\delta\) definition that \[ \lim_{x\to 1}\frac{x^2-1}{x-1}=2. \]
Prove using the \(\varepsilon\)-\(\delta\) definition that \[ \lim_{x\to 0}x^2=0. \]
Prove that for any \(a,c\in\mathbb{R}\), \[ \lim_{x\to a}c=c,\qquad \lim_{x\to a}x=a. \]
State the sum, product, and quotient rules for limits. Give one example of each.
Compute \[ \lim_{x\to 3}\frac{x^2-9}{x-3}. \]
Compute \[ \lim_{x\to 0}\frac{\sqrt{1+x}-1}{x}. \]
Compute \[ \lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{3x^2+2x}{5x^2-1}. \]
Show that \[ \lim_{x\to 0}\sin\left(\frac{1}{x}\right) \] does not exist.
Use the sequential criterion for limits to show that \[ \lim_{x\to 0}x\sin\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)=0. \]
Find the left-hand and right-hand limits of \[ f(x)=\frac{|x|}{x} \] at \(x=0\). Does \(\lim_{x\to 0}f(x)\) exist?
Prove that if \(\lim_{x\to a}f(x)\) exists, then it is unique.
Use the squeeze theorem to prove that \[ \lim_{x\to 0}x^2\sin\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)=0. \]

Part D: Introduction to Riemann Integration

Define a partition \(P\) of \([a,b]\), the lower Riemann sum \(L(P,f)\), and the upper Riemann sum \(U(P,f)\).
Define what it means for a bounded function \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb{R}\) to be Riemann integrable.
Let \(f(x)=x\) on \([0,1]\). For the uniform partition \[ P_n=\left\{0,\frac{1}{n},\frac{2}{n},\dots,1\right\}, \] compute \(L(P_n,f)\) and \(U(P_n,f)\).
Using the previous question, show that \(f(x)=x\) is Riemann integrable on \([0,1]\) and find \[ \int_0^1 x\,dx. \]
Let \(f(x)=c\) be constant on \([a,b]\). For any partition \(P\) of \([a,b]\), compute \(L(P,f)\) and \(U(P,f)\). Hence find \[ \int_a^b c\,dx. \]
Let \(f(x)=x^2\) on \([0,1]\). For the uniform partition \(P_n\), compute \(L(P_n,f)\) and \(U(P_n,f)\). Find their limits as \(n\to\infty\).
Let \[ f(x)= \begin{cases} 1, & x\in\mathbb{Q},\\ 0, & x\notin\mathbb{Q}, \end{cases} \qquad x\in[0,1]. \] Compute \(L(P,f)\) and \(U(P,f)\) for any partition \(P\) of \([0,1]\). Is \(f\) Riemann integrable?
Let \(f(x)=x\) on \([0,2]\) and let \[ P=\{0,1,2\}. \] Compute \(L(P,f)\) and \(U(P,f)\).
Let \(P'\) be a refinement of a partition \(P\) of \([a,b]\). Prove that \[ L(P,f)\le L(P',f)\le U(P',f)\le U(P,f). \]
State the Cauchy criterion for Riemann integrability.
Prove that if \(f:[a,b]\to\mathbb{R}\) is monotone, then \(f\) is Riemann integrable.
If \(f\) is Riemann integrable on \([a,b]\), is \(|f|\) necessarily Riemann integrable? Justify briefly.
Evaluate \[ \int_0^1 (2x+1)\,dx \] directly from the definition using lower and upper sums.
Let \(f(x)=x\) on \([0,1]\), except define \(f(1/2)=100\). Show that \(f\) is still Riemann integrable and \[ \int_0^1 f(x)\,dx=\frac12. \]
Prove that if \(f\) is Riemann integrable on \([a,b]\) and \(c\in\mathbb{R}\), then \(cf\) is Riemann integrable on \([a,b]\) and \[ \int_a^b c\,f(x)\,dx = c\int_a^b f(x)\,dx. \]
Let \(f(x)=e^x\) on \([0,1]\). For the uniform partition \(P_n\), write \(L(P_n,f)\) and \(U(P_n,f)\) as sums.
Let \(f(x)=x^2\) on \([0,1]\) and let \[ P=\left\{0,\frac14,\frac12,1\right\}. \] Compute \(L(P,f)\) and \(U(P,f)\).
Prove that if \(f\) is Riemann integrable on \([a,b]\) and \(f(x)\ge 0\) for all \(x\in[a,b]\), then \[ \int_a^b f(x)\,dx\ge 0. \]
Give an example of a bounded function on \([0,1]\) that is not Riemann integrable. Explain why it fails.
Let \(f(x)=\lfloor x\rfloor\) on \([0,3]\). For the partition \[ P=\{0,1,2,3\}, \] compute \(L(P,f)\) and \(U(P,f)\). Is \(f\) Riemann integrable on \([0,3]\)? If so, find \[ \int_0^3 f(x)\,dx. \]
